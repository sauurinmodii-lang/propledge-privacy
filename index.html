What stays on your phone

Propledge is a property register for brokers. The records you enter (owner names, phone numbers, addresses, amounts, remarks, photos, videos and documents), your firm details, your logo and your settings are stored only on your phone. We do not run a server, we have no accounts, and we cannot see your records. Backups you create are saved where you choose (for example a folder on your phone or a cloud-drive folder you select); we do not receive them.

Permissions

Internet and network state: used only to show ads and to check for app updates through Google Play. Camera: only when you choose to take a photo or video inside a property (through the system camera). Files and contacts are picked through the Android system pickers; the app does not get broad storage or contacts access. Fingerprint/face unlock, if you turn it on, is handled by Android; the app never receives your biometric data.

Advertising

The free app shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob on the main screens. AdMob (Google) may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change your choice later in Settings. Ads are limited to family-friendly content, and the app is not directed at children. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Updates and diagnostics

Google Play may collect crash and performance data under its own terms. Propledge itself does not include analytics.

Data we collect

We, the developer, collect no personal data from the app. Data handled by the ad SDK is described above.

Your records and other people's data

The records you enter may contain other people's personal details (for example property owners). You are responsible for having a lawful reason to keep and share them. Deleting the app deletes its data from the phone; keep your own backups.

Children

Propledge is not intended for children under 18.
